# Wake flotsam · crate, barrel and plank
An original noncolliding, unlit decoration kit with one parent and exact child names `crate`, `barrel`, `plank`. The parent presents a compact group, but each child has centered local geometry and can be cloned singly for sparsely placed accents. All pieces use a wet-waterline center and dull wood/iron colors; no signal lights and no textures. Their submerged portions should remain mostly hidden under the water shader. Keep a few small groups near the bank, or outside the turn corridor on the edge of a searchlight area. Do not spawn them onto the primary steer lane or mark them as gameplay objects.

Build: `blender -b -P art/sources/debris/build.py`. Budget excludes any duplicate decoration instances managed by the renderer.
